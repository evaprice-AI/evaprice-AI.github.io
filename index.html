About

I'm a final year PhD candidate at University College London, working at the intersection of biological science and machine learning. My thesis, "Next Generation Viral Vector Design via Liquid Machine Learning Reinforced with Pan-omics," applies ML and multi-omics data integration to inform the engineering of cell lines used in viral vector production for gene therapy.

I build scalable, reproducible workflows for large-scale data analysis — leveraging high-performance computing environments including AWS and Nextflow — for efficient, transparent research. I'm committed to data stewardship, open science, and making computational methods accessible and reproducible for everyone.
